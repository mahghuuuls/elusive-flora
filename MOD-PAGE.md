This mod adds rare plants that grow only in specific places. Each kind has its own biomes and its own spot: the sea bed, a cliff face, a tree trunk, desert sand, the edge of swamp water, snow, a lava shore, the Nether, the End, the Aether. Some kinds open only at night, in rain, at a full or new moon, or in a season. A journal, the Wanderer's Herbarium, says where and when each plant grows, and lists the biomes. A picked plant leaves a stem that grows back in time. Plants cannot be moved, planted, or farmed.

The mod adds no recipes and no uses for the picked plants. It is a base for other mods, which can add crafting or uses for them.

<span style="color:#d6a100">**AI usage disclaimer:** This mod was developed with AI-agent assistance using [this agent workflow](https://github.com/mahghuuuls/minecraft-1.12.2-mod-agent-workflow). The project owner reviewed the work during development.</span>

- Plants are placed when a chunk first generates, one plant at a time and far apart.
- Hit a plant once to pick it. It drops one of itself and leaves a stem. The stem grows back into the plant after a while, by default three hours of real time, faster or slower by kind.
- Break the stem and that spot is gone for good.
- Some plants glow. Some are closed by day, in clear weather, or out of their season, and open again when their time comes.
- Craft the journal from a book and a dandelion. Every plant has three pages: the plant, when it is open and how long it takes to grow back, and where it grows.

**Plants and fungi :)**

Overworld:

- Ledgebloom: on cliff faces in mountains and hills.
- Pearlfrond: on the deep sea bed. Glows.
- Streamreed: on river beds.
- Tideheart: on the shallow sea bed near the shore.
- Skyclover: high on mountains.
- Canopy Tear: on tree trunks in jungles.
- Frostbell: in gaps of the snow cover in snowy biomes.
- Sporecap: on mycelium in mushroom biomes.
- Emberroot: beside surface lava, in any biome.
- Duskwisp: in forests. Opens at night and glows.
- Rainthistle: in swamps and wet biomes. Opens in rain.
- Thawbud: in plains and forests. Opens in spring with Serene Seasons.
- Sunspire: in savannas, plains, and hot biomes. Opens in summer with Serene Seasons.
- Amberleaf: in forests. Opens in autumn with Serene Seasons.
- Winterthorn: in cold biomes. Opens in winter with Serene Seasons.
- Moonveil: in magical, lush, and forest biomes. Opens at full moon and glows.
- Umbrabud: in spooky, dead, and swamp biomes. Opens at new moon.
- Dunerose: on desert and mesa sand.
- Gravebell: in spooky woods. Glows faintly.
- Mournlily: on the edge of swamp water.

Nether:

- Soulwick: on soul sand.
- Ashen Lotus: on netherrack beside lava.
- Blazecap: in the fungus biomes of Biomes O' Plenty, BetterNether, and Nether Backport. Glows.
- Wartvine: in the garden biomes of Biomes O' Plenty and BetterNether.
- Marrowbloom: in the bone biomes of BetterNether, Nether Backport, and Biomes O' Plenty.

End:

- Voidbloom: anywhere in the End. Glows.
- Starpetal: in a few biomes of Better End Forge, Stygian End, and Environs. Glows.
- Sporelantern: in the fungus biomes of Better End Forge and Stygian End. Glows.
- Shadelace: in the dark biomes of Better End Forge.
- Prismthorn: in the crystal biomes of Better End Forge. Glows.
- Deepglow: by the lakes and springs of Better End Forge. Glows.

Aether:

- Cloudfern: on Aether grass.

**Other mods**

- [Patchouli](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/patchouli) is required for the journal. Version 1.0-28 or newer.
- [Serene Seasons](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/serene-seasons) is optional. With it, four plants open only in their season. Without it, they are always open.
- [The Aether](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/aether-legacy), Better End Forge, Stygian End, Environs, BetterNether, Nether Backport, and [Biomes O' Plenty](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/biomes-o-plenty) are optional. Nine plants grow only in biomes those mods add. Without them, those plants exist but never grow, and their journal pages say so.
